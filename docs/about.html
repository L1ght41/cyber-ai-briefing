About Signal & Shield

A short, sourced morning read on what changed in cybersecurity and AI, and why it matters to the people who defend systems.

Signal & Shield is published every morning by Simon Boadi, a cybersecurity engineer working across cloud security, DevSecOps and AI governance. Each briefing covers the most significant security stories (breaches, actively exploited vulnerabilities, threat activity and policy) alongside the AI developments that shape how software is built and secured.

Every item links to its original reporting. Summaries are written for practitioners: what happened, and why it matters.

Briefings are researched and drafted with AI assistance from public reporting and published automatically, so always check the linked sources before acting on anything here.
